      Our core requirements

      • 01A beautiful, scenic resort — natural beauty doing the decorating, not a ballroom we have to dress up ourselves.
      • 02Taquiza with a live taquero — a real taco station, not a 3-course plated dinner standing in for it.
      • 03Mariachi & live music — outside entertainment welcome, no vendor lockout.
      • 04A late-night reception — no early noise curfew forcing the party indoors or shut down by 11.
      • 05Papel picado overhead — strung over the reception in our six-color palette, not the default red-white-green.
      • 06Fashion — a backdrop worthy of the dress code, so loud color and 70s tailoring pop instead of fighting a beige ballroom.
